Barista Training

Master the Art of the Pour

From your first shot of espresso to competition-level latte art — hands-on training taught the way we actually work the bar, right here in Birtamod.

A hands-on, specialty-focused curriculum for anyone who wants to understand coffee more deeply — or make a career of it. Three tiers, from complete beginner to competition prep, taught on the same bar we brew on every day.

Photos and videos from training sessions coming soon.

  • Beginner Barista — coffee theory, espresso basics, intro to milk texturing
  • Intermediate Brewing & Latte Art — espresso calibration, latte art, manual brewing (V60, AeroPress)
  • Advanced & Competition Prep — advanced extraction, signature drink development, competition coaching

Our Roof

Why Ouz
Coffee & Co.

From the bean to the cup to the classroom, everything we do traces back to the same five commitments — sourcing it right, teaching it properly, equipping it well, and building something worth being part of.

01 — Expertise

Specialty Coffee Expertise

We source and roast top-quality beans from Nepal and abroad, with the precision to keep every batch consistent — backed by baristas and trainers skilled enough to make sure of it.

02 — Connection

Local & Global Connection

We support Nepali farmers and local coffee culture while blending in international standards — a Nepali coffee experience for locals and travelers alike.

03 — Education

Comprehensive Coffee Education

Hands-on training from beginner to professional — brewing, espresso, roasting, and latte art — for baristas, enthusiasts, and businesses alike.

04 — Gear

Premium Coffee Gear & Accessories

In partnership with Brewing House, we offer curated brewing gear — grinders, kettles, cups, and accessories — to recreate specialty coffee at home.

05 — Community

Passion for Craft & Community

Coffee is an experience, not just a drink. We're building community and crafting every cup, class, and product with care.
