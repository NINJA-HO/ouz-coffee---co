Our Green Coffee

Grown on our own farm in the Ilam hills.

Our green coffee comes from our own farm in the lower Ilam region, near the Ilam–Jhapa transition belt, at 800–1,200m — a microclimate that blends Terai warmth with hill elevation for balanced Arabica growing conditions. It's shade-grown alongside banana and avocado trees, on about 1.69 hectares managed for long-term soil health rather than maximum yield.

That means every bag we sell is traceable to a specific plot we farm ourselves, not sourced anonymously from a broker. Curious about the fuller story — cultivation, processing, and where Nepali coffee is headed? Read Green Coffee in Nepal.

Natural Process

Sun-Dried Cherry

Handpicked ripe cherries dried on raised beds, so the bean absorbs sugars from the fruit — fruity, sweet, full-bodied lots.

Washed Process

Clean & Bright

Pulped and fermented to remove mucilage before washing and drying — clarity, brightness, and crisp acidity.

Limited Availability

Anaerobic Micro-Lots

Sealed-fermentation experimental lots with complex, wine-like notes — produced in small, carefully monitored batches.

Farm Capacity

What's actually available each season.

Because this comes from one farm we manage directly, supply is genuinely limited — 400–500 kg of green coffee a year under standard management, rising to about 700 kg with more advanced practices. That's part of why lots sell out and rotate by harvest and processing method, rather than staying in stock year-round.
